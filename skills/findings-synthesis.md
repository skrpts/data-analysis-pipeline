## Capability

Reads the findings from both strands and identifies where they converge, diverge, or complement one another, producing one integrated account rather than two parallel ones.

## When to Use

- Reporting a mixed-methods study
- Reconciling a statistical result with what participants actually said
- Deciding which strand carries the weight of a contested claim

## Inputs

Quantitative findings, qualitative findings, research questions, integration approach

## Outputs

Integrated findings narrative naming the points of convergence, divergence and complementarity
